Add your images here. PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, and SVG are supported.

- logo.png: your logo; transparent PNG or SVG works best.
- home-page-img.jpg: your full-screen homepage photo.

The app also recognizes the other supported extensions for these names.
Use lowercase filenames and extensions. Refresh the page after adding files.
